About Me - Behind The Tickets!

I'm a Senior Automation Support Engineer with 2+ years at BrowserStack, sitting at the intersection of technical support and automation engineering. I help enterprise customers troubleshoot and unblock complex Selenium, Playwright, and Appium etc pipelines, while also building the internal tools — AI-powered ticket populating system, New Ticket Notifier — that helps the team monitoring the queue and responding to issues faster. My work is driven by a passion for automation and problem-solving, and I thrive in environments where I can combine both to deliver impactful solutions.

I enjoy root-causing failures across distributed systems as much as I enjoy scripting the fix that prevents them from recurring. I built a Replay Studio UI for a CLI-based system that helps teams reproduce issues quickly — making debugging and root-cause analysis a lot faster. Currently learning AI tools and LLMs — how they work under the hood, RAG, and everything in between.
