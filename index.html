About Me - Behind The Ticket!

I'm a Senior Solutions Engineer with 2+ years at BrowserStack, sitting at the intersection of technical support and automation engineering. I help enterprise customers troubleshoot and unblock complex Selenium, Playwright, and Appium pipelines, while also building the internal tooling — AI-powered ticket automation, shift-aware notifiers — that makes support teams faster.

I enjoy root-causing failures across distributed systems as much as I enjoy scripting the fix that prevents them from recurring. Outside of support engineering, I build end-to-end automation systems for fun: trading bots, YouTube content pipelines, and full-stack web apps — all driven by the same instinct to turn a manual, repetitive process into something that just runs on its own.
